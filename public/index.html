    Before we look that up

    FridgeFuse worked out your area from its own store list, entirely on this machine. To show a real place name instead, it would send your coordinates — rounded to about 110 metres — to OpenStreetMap's free Nominatim service. That is a third party outside this app.

    Say no and nothing leaves your machine. Store distances and prices are calculated locally either way, so the comparison works exactly the same.
